# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive community for everyone.

Expected behavior includes:

- Respectful and constructive communication
- Welcoming different viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and receiving feedback professionally
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and other conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior by contacting the repository maintainers privately. Public issues are not appropriate for conduct reports.

Maintainers will review reports fairly and take any action they consider appropriate.
